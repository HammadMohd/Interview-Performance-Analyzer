================================================================================
                    INTERVIEW PERFORMANCE ANALYZER
                       FEATURE DESCRIPTIONS
================================================================================

This document describes all features used in the Interview Performance Analyzer
project across two main datasets: merged_features.csv and target_features.csv.

================================================================================
SECTION 1: METADATA & IDENTITY FEATURES
================================================================================

id                  - Unique numeric identifier for each interview segment
file_name           - Video file name (e.g., vid_0001.mp4)
duration_label      - Categorical label for video length: "short", "medium", or "long"
question_id         - Numeric ID of the interview question being answered
question            - Text of the interview question (e.g., "Introduce yourself")
user_no             - Numeric identifier for the interviewee

================================================================================
SECTION 2: PERSONALITY TRAITS (Big Five / OCEAN Model)
================================================================================

These are standardized Z-scores (mean=0, std=1) representing personality
dimensions based on the Big Five personality model.

openness            - Openness to experience. High = curious, creative, adventurous.
                      Low = practical, conventional, prefers routine.

conscientiousness   - Conscientiousness. High = organized, dependable, disciplined.
                      Low = flexible, spontaneous, careless.

extraversion        - Extraversion. High = outgoing, energetic, talkative.
                      Low = introverted, reserved, solitary.

agreeableness       - Agreeableness. High = cooperative, trusting, empathetic.
                      Low = competitive, skeptical, challenging.

neuroticism         - Neuroticism (Emotional Stability). High = anxious, moody,
                      emotionally reactive. Low = calm, resilient, stable.

overall_personality - Aggregate personality score combining all five traits.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: INTERVIEW SCORING (Target Variables)
================================================================================

These are the target labels used for predicting interview performance.
Scores are standardized Z-scores.

interview_score     - Overall interview performance score (primary target).

--- Additional targets in target_features.csv only ---

answer_score        - Quality/relevance score of the verbal answer given.
speaking_skills     - Score for fluency, articulation, and speech clarity.
confidence_score    - Score reflecting perceived confidence during delivery.
facial_expression   - Score for appropriateness and expressiveness of facial cues.
overall_performance - Comprehensive score combining all performance dimensions.

================================================================================
SECTION 4: AUDIO / SPEECH FEATURES
================================================================================

Duration_Sec        - Total duration of the speech segment in seconds.
Silence_Duration_Sec- Total duration of silence/pauses within the speech.
Speech_Rate_WPM     - Speaking rate in Words Per Minute (WPM).
                      Normal conversation: 120-150 WPM.
Mean_Pitch_Hz       - Average fundamental frequency (F0) of the voice in Hz.
                      Higher pitch may indicate nervousness or excitement.
Mean_Energy         - Average energy/amplitude of the audio signal.
                      Higher energy suggests louder, more assertive speech.
Mean_ZCR            - Mean Zero Crossing Rate. Measures how often the signal
                      crosses zero. Related to noisiness and speech characteristics.

================================================================================
SECTION 5: MFCC FEATURES (Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients)
================================================================================

MFCC features capture the spectral envelope of the audio signal, representing
the shape of the vocal tract. They are widely used in speech recognition
and emotion detection.

MFCC_1 through MFCC_13 - 13 cepstral coefficients capturing spectral features.
  MFCC_1               - Typically captures overall spectral energy/loudness.
  MFCC_2-4             - Capture broad spectral shape and voice characteristics.
  MFCC_5-8             - Capture mid-frequency details and formant structure.
  MFCC_9-13            - Capture finer spectral details and noise characteristics.

================================================================================
SECTION 6: TRANSCRIPT FEATURES
================================================================================

transcript          - Full text transcript of the speech with timestamps.
                      Format: [MM:SS - MM:SS] spoken text.
                      Used for NLP analysis, sentiment, and content evaluation.

================================================================================
SECTION 7: FACE DETECTION FEATURES
================================================================================

face_detected_ratio - Proportion of video frames where a face was detected.
                      Range: 0.0 to 1.0. Higher = more consistent face visibility.

================================================================================
SECTION 8: GAZE / EYE TRACKING FEATURES
================================================================================

These features track where the candidate is looking, which indicates
confidence, engagement, and honesty.

gaze_ratio_mean     - Average horizontal gaze position (0 = left, 0.5 = center,
                      1 = right). Value near 0.5 suggests direct eye contact
                      with camera/interviewer.

gaze_deviation_mean - Average deviation of gaze from center.
                      Lower = more steady eye contact.
                      Higher = looking around frequently.

gaze_stability_std  - Standard deviation of gaze position over time.
                      Lower = more stable, focused gaze.
                      Higher = erratic eye movement (possible nervousness).

================================================================================
SECTION 9: FACIAL EXPRESSION - MOUTH FEATURES
================================================================================

These capture mouth-related expressions using facial landmark detection.

smile_score_mean    - Average smile intensity score (0 to 1).
                      Higher = more smiling, suggesting warmth and friendliness.

smile_score_std     - Standard deviation of smile intensity.
                      Higher = more variation in smiling (natural expressions).

frown_score_mean    - Average frown intensity score.
                      Higher = more frowning, suggesting discomfort or confusion.

frown_score_std     - Standard deviation of frown intensity.
                      Higher = more variation in frowning.

================================================================================
SECTION 10: FACIAL EXPRESSION - EYE & BROW FEATURES
================================================================================

eye_openness_mean   - Average eye openness ratio.
                      Higher = eyes more open (alert, attentive).
                      Lower = eyes more closed (drowsy, disengaged).

eye_openness_std    - Standard deviation of eye openness.
                      Higher = more blinking or expression variation.

jaw_open_mean       - Average jaw opening distance.
                      Higher = mouth opens wider (speaking actively).
                      Lower = jaw mostly closed.

jaw_open_std        - Standard deviation of jaw opening.
                      Higher = more dynamic mouth movement while speaking.

brow_raise_mean     - Average eyebrow raise intensity.
                      Higher = raised eyebrows (surprise, emphasis, engagement).

brow_raise_std      - Standard deviation of eyebrow raise.
                      Higher = more expressive eyebrow movement.

mouth_frown_mean    - Average mouth frown intensity (downward lip corners).
                      Higher = more downturned mouth (displeasure, nervousness).

mouth_frown_std     - Standard deviation of mouth frown.
                      Higher = more variation in mouth frown expression.

================================================================================
SECTION 11: EMOTION DETECTION FEATURES
================================================================================

These are average probabilities (0 to 1) from an emotion recognition model
applied to facial expressions across all detected frames.

emotion_happy_mean  - Probability of happiness expression.
emotion_sad_mean    - Probability of sadness expression.
emotion_angry_mean  - Probability of anger expression.
emotion_surprise_mean - Probability of surprise expression.
emotion_fear_mean   - Probability of fear/anxiety expression.
emotion_disgust_mean - Probability of disgust expression.
emotion_neutral_mean - Probability of neutral/calm expression.

Higher values indicate the model detected more of that emotion.

================================================================================
SECTION 12: HEAD & UPPER BODY POSTURE FEATURES
================================================================================

These features measure body language, posture, and upper body movement
using pose estimation (e.g., MediaPipe or OpenPose).

head_centering_score_mean - How centered the head is in the video frame.
                      Higher = head more centered (confident, engaged).
                      Lower = head off-center (leaning, distracted).

absolute_shoulder_slope_mean - Average absolute tilt of the shoulders.
                      Lower = shoulders more level (calm, confident).
                      Higher = shoulders more tilted (tense, asymmetric posture).

shoulder_slope_var   - Variance in shoulder tilt over time.
                      Lower = more stable posture.
                      Higher = more body movement/fidgeting.

shoulder_width_mean  - Average apparent shoulder width in the frame.
                      May relate to posture openness (wider = more open).

shoulder_width_var   - Variance in shoulder width.
                      Higher = more body rotation or shifting.

nose_shoulder_dist_mean - Average distance between nose and shoulder landmarks.
                      May indicate head tilt or posture.

nose_shoulder_dist_var - Variance in nose-shoulder distance.
                      Higher = more head movement relative to shoulders.

================================================================================
SECTION 13: HAND & ARM GESTURE FEATURES
================================================================================

hand_speed_mean     - Average speed of hand movements (pixels/frame).
                      Higher = more animated hand gestures.
                      Lower = hands mostly still.

hand_to_face_touches - Count of times hands touched the face during the video.
                      Higher = more face touching (possible nervousness or habit).

crossed_arms_score  - Score indicating how often arms were crossed.
                      Higher = more crossed arms (defensive or closed posture).

================================================================================
SECTION 14: CORE BODY MOVEMENT FEATURES
================================================================================

core_speed_mean     - Average speed of torso/core body movement.
                      Higher = more body movement (restlessness or energy).
                      Lower = more still and composed.

posture_shift_count - Number of significant posture changes/shifts.
                      Higher = more shifting (discomfort or restlessness).
                      Lower = more stable, composed posture.

================================================================================
SECTION 15: BEHAVIORAL SCORE FEATURES
================================================================================

engagement_score    - Composite score measuring overall engagement level.
                      Combines gaze, facial expression, and body language cues.
                      Higher = more engaged and attentive.

agitation_score     - Composite score measuring nervousness or agitation.
                      Combines fidgeting, gaze instability, and facial cues.
                      Higher = more agitated or nervous.

================================================================================
SECTION 17: DATA NOTES
================================================================================

1. All personality and scoring features are standardized Z-scores centered
   around 0 with unit variance. Negative values are below average, positive
   values are above average.

2. Audio features (MFCC, pitch, energy, ZCR) are extracted per-segment.
   Face and posture features are averaged across all detected frames.

3. Some rows may have missing values in certain features (e.g., no transcript
   if speech was not detected, or face_detected_ratio < 1 if face was lost).

4. The target_features.csv contains additional scoring dimensions not present
   in merged_features.csv, suggesting these may come from manual annotation
   or a different scoring pipeline.
